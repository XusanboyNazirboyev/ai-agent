# ADR 061 — Adding a lead by hand in the funnel (manual leads)

Status: proposed by the reviewing agent on 2026-11-13 after the owner's request ("a lead can be added in the funnel too", as in every CRM: a phone call, a walk-in, a referral). Builds on ADR 052 (Person), 057/058 (pipelines, funnel, stage lock).

## Context (read from the code on 2026-11-13)
Every lead is created by the agent from a conversation: `leads.contact_id` and `leads.conversation_id` are NOT NULL (composite FKs to `contacts` and `conversations`), and a contact needs a channel account and a provider user id. Consumers assume them: the lead list joins the contact (name, phone) and the conversation (`channelType` badge, "Open conversation"), CRM sync reads the contact, contact erasure and person merge walk `contacts -> person`, the CSV export reads the person. A nullable-everywhere schema change would touch all of these.

## Decisions
1. **No schema change.** A manual lead is a normal lead whose conversation is an internal, closed one on a per-tenant internal channel account. Created together, in one transaction:
   - the tenant's **internal channel account** (get-or-create): `channel_type = 'manual'`, `external_account_id = 'manual'`, `status = DISABLED`, `agent_mode = OFF`; it never receives webhooks, is never polled, never connected or health-checked, and is hidden from the Channels page, channel counts/limits, connectivity checks and any "channels" listing (grep every place that lists or iterates channel accounts and exclude `channel_type = 'manual'`);
   - a **contact** on it: `provider_user_id = 'manual:<uuid>'`, name and normalised phone from the request (use the existing phone normalisation, `leads/phone.ts` / persons code), linked to a **person** exactly like contacts of other channels: if a live person with the same normalised phone exists in the tenant, link to it (no duplicate customer, ADR 052), otherwise create one (use the existing person-linking helper, `persons/ensure-contact.ts` and `people.service.ts`: read them first);
   - a **conversation**: `state = CLOSED`, `provider_chat_id = 'manual:<uuid>'`, no messages; the bot is silent on a closed conversation and nothing ever addresses it;
   - the **lead**: pipeline and stage from the request (the stage must belong to the pipeline; default: the tenant's default pipeline and its first open stage), `stage_set_by = OPERATOR` (an operator created it: the AI must not restage it), `source` = the request's value or `manual`, `status` per the stage kind (open stage -> NEW), `score` default, `summary` = the optional note (may be empty), `idempotency_key = 'manual:<uuid>'`, opportunity fields as in ADR 057 (amount and currency together, owner an active operator of the tenant; default owner = the creating operator), CRM sync status PENDING as for any lead (a manual lead reaches Bitrix24 like others; check `lead-sync.service.ts` copes with an empty summary and a manual channel).
2. **API.** `POST /api/v1/leads` body `{ name, phone?, pipelineId?, stageId?, amount?, currency?, source?, ownerOperatorId?, note?, tagIds? }`, strict zod: `name` trimmed 1..200; `phone` optional, normalised, invalid -> 400 `INVALID_VALUE`; `note` max 1000; `source` trimmed max 60; `amount` decimal string `^\d{1,12}(\.\d{1,2})?$` with `currency` (both or neither, ISO list as in ADR 057); unknown/other-tenant pipeline, stage, owner, tag -> 404 `NOT_FOUND` / 400 `UNKNOWN_OPERATOR` / `UNKNOWN_TAG`; a stage of kind LOST requires nothing here (a lead is never created closed: reject `WON`/`LOST` stages with 400 `STAGE_NOT_OPEN`). Roles OWNER, ADMIN, OPERATOR; VIEWER 403. Response: the same item shape as `GET /leads` for that lead (`LeadPipelineItem`), so the board can insert it at once. Audit `lead.created_manually` (ids only: lead, stage, pipeline; no name/phone). Rate-limit like other write routes if the codebase has a shared guard; otherwise a per-operator limit is out of scope.
3. **Reading manual leads.** Everything that already reads leads keeps working. Additions: the lead item's `conversation.channelType` is `'manual'` for these leads (the web shows the label "Manual" and hides "Open conversation"); the Inbox lists exclude them already because their conversation is CLOSED and empty, but verify that the "closed" scope does not show an empty manual conversation: exclude `channel_type = 'manual'` conversations from every inbox scope.
4. **Dedup and privacy.** Two manual leads for the same phone share the person (decision 1); contact erasure and person erasure treat the manual contact like any other (ADR 049/052), no special cases; the CSV export needs no change (it reads the person and lead fields).
5. **Web (separate step, after the board redesign settles):** an "Add lead" button in the Leads toolbar and a "+" at the top of every open column (the column's stage is preselected) open a dialog: name (required), phone, amount + currency, source (default "Manual"), owner (default: me; managers see the operator select as in the lead panel), note; on success the card appears in its column and the panel may open.

## Staging of the work
S1 API (builder; reviewer verifies): the route, the internal channel account and the exclusions, the tests (unit for validation; integration: creates account+contact+conversation+lead in one transaction, idempotent account, phone dedup links to the existing person, stage/pipeline mismatch, WON/LOST stage rejected, owner validation, role matrix, cross-tenant 404, the lead appears in `GET /leads` and in `GET /pipelines/:id/board` counts, the agent never restages it (OPERATOR lock), inbox scopes and the channels listing do not show the manual account/conversation, erasure of the manual contact works, CSV export includes it, CRM sync of an empty-summary manual lead). S2 web (Kilo or builder) after S1 and after the board swipe work lands.

## Risks
The internal channel account must never be mistaken for a real one: every place that iterates channel accounts needs the exclusion (a missed place would show a phantom "manual" channel or try to poll it); the builder greps and lists each place it checked. The person-linking helper must be used, not re-implemented, or duplicates appear.

## S1 status (2026-11-13, branch `feat/manual-leads`, API built and verified by the reviewing agent)

Built: `POST /api/v1/leads` (`webinbox/lead-pipeline.controller.ts`, roles OWNER/ADMIN/OPERATOR, VIEWER 403, strict body, response = the `GET /leads` item shape); `leads/manual-lead.service.ts` (one transaction: internal channel account, contact linked to a person by phone key or a new person, CLOSED conversation, lead with `stage_set_by = OPERATOR`, tags, audit `lead.created_manually` with ids only); pure rules in `leads/manual-lead-rules.ts`; the list mapper of `GET /leads` is shared (`LEAD_ITEM_SELECT`, `toItems`).

Deviations from the text above:
- The internal account's `external_account_id` is `manual:<tenantId>` (not `manual`): `channel_accounts` is unique on `(channel_type, external_account_id)` across ALL tenants.
- Phone "normalisation" is the existing `extractPhone` (digits, a leading `+` kept, 9 to 15 digits, nothing completed); the person link uses `phoneKey` (last 9 digits), so `+998 90 111 22 33` and `90 111 22 33` meet one person.
- Places excluding `channel_type = 'manual'`: inbox list (every scope, `inbox-read.service.ts`), channels list and agent-mode PATCH (`channels.controller.ts`), platform tenant detail channels (`platform-tenants.controller.ts`). Checked and left alone because the account is `DISABLED`/`agent_mode OFF` and conversations are CLOSED: operator digest and alerts scans (need `ACTIVE` + `operator_chat_id`), follow-ups (Telegram only), agent startup check (one served account id), channel connect controllers (filter by their own type). No plan limit counts channel accounts.
- CRM sync passes `summary` through unchanged; an empty summary was read, not run against a live Bitrix24.

Tests: unit `manual-lead-rules.spec.ts` (8); integration `webinbox/manual-lead.integration.spec.ts` (12: defaults, one account per tenant, person dedup, validation, amount/owner/tags, owner and tag rejection, stage/pipeline rules, role matrix, list + board count + audit without personal data, inbox/channels exclusion, operator lock). Five mutations were killed (inbox filter, channels filter, `stage_set_by`, closed-stage rule, person link). Not covered: contact/person erasure and CSV export of a manual lead (no special cases in code, not exercised), CRM sync of an empty summary against a real connector.

S2 web (the "Add lead" button and column "+" dialog; label "Manual", no "Open conversation" for `channelType = 'manual'`) is the next step.
