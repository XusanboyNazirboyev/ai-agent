# ADR 060 — Knowledge-only tenants: the agent answers without a catalog (AUD-09, AUD-14)

Status: proposed by the reviewing agent on 2026-11-13 (external audit tasks AUD-09 and AUD-14). Builds on D1/D2/D5 (facts from tools, RAG only for text), ADR 007 (connector contract), ADR 059 (connector roles: an unbound or unavailable `CATALOG` role yields no connector).

## Context (read from the code on 2026-11-13)
- `packages/shared/src/domain-pack.ts:305` rejects every Domain Pack whose `tools` do not include `search_catalog` ("search_catalog is required"). A business that has no product/price catalog (a clinic, a school with FAQ and opening hours, a law office) cannot be described by a pack.
- `apps/api/src/core/agent/agent-turn.ts:813-817`: a normal turn (not regulated, not scheduling) whose tool set lacks `search_catalog` ends in `handover('NO_DATA')`. A tenant with no catalog connector, or whose `CATALOG` role is unbound/ambiguous/unavailable (ADR 059), therefore hands over EVERY customer question, including "what are your opening hours?" that its knowledge base answers. The knowledge path exists only for `regulated_info` questions (`agent-turn.ts:739-806`), where it demands a regulated passage that is still valid.
- `knowledge_search` already produces facts of kind `knowledge` with provenance (`core/agent/tools.ts:1045`, `facts.ts:40`), so a reply can be rendered from them under the existing guardrails (D5).

## Decisions
1. **A pack declares what it needs.** `tools` must contain `search_catalog` OR `knowledge_search` (validation message: "a pack needs search_catalog or knowledge_search"). A pack that lists only `knowledge_search` is a *knowledge-only pack*. Packs that list `search_catalog` behave exactly as today. No other pack field changes; existing packs (education, travel) and their tests are untouched.
2. **A "knowledge turn" for catalog-less operation.** In `agent-turn.ts`, when the tool set has no `search_catalog` but has `knowledge_search`, a normal (non-regulated, non-scheduling) turn does NOT hand over `NO_DATA`; it runs the same early search as the regulated branch: `tools.searchKnowledge(customerText)`; the outcome is `FOUND` only if at least one fact of kind `knowledge` was returned. Then the existing reply pipeline renders the answer from those facts (same guardrails, same provenance requirements, same shown-fact tracking). The "regulated, still valid" check applies only to `regulated_info` turns, not to ordinary knowledge turns.
3. **What a knowledge turn must NOT do (D1/D5 stay intact).** It never states a price, availability, date or seat count: those exist only as catalog/offer facts. If the customer's message asks for such a fact (intent `price`, `availability`, `booking`... see the intent list in `core/agent`), or the knowledge search finds nothing, the turn hands over with the existing reason `NO_DATA`. A knowledge passage that itself contains a number (e.g. "open 9:00-18:00") may be quoted ONLY through the existing knowledge-fact rendering (the guardrail already treats knowledge facts as provenance-carrying); do not add a new number path.
4. **Missing slots.** Slots whose `requiredFor` includes `search` are catalog-search slots. In a knowledge turn they are not asked (there is no search to fill them for); slots required for other purposes (lead capture, booking) are asked as today.
5. **Selection stays deterministic (ADR 059).** "No catalog tool" means `selectConnector(tenantId, 'CATALOG')` returned `undefined` (no capable connector, ambiguous, or bound-but-unavailable). In that case a pack that also offers `knowledge_search` runs knowledge turns; a pack that does not still hands over `NO_DATA` as today. The agent logs `agent.catalog_unavailable` once per turn (tenant, pack, reason: `NO_CONNECTOR|AMBIGUOUS|UNAVAILABLE|PACK_HAS_NO_CATALOG`; no customer text).
6. **Evaluation (D14).** Add an eval scenario set for a knowledge-only pack (opening hours, address, policy question answered from a knowledge passage; price question -> handover; unknown question -> handover; a prompt-injection attempt in the customer text or in a knowledge passage does not change tool use) in the existing eval harness style for the offline (fake provider) suite; no paid model calls.
7. **Out of scope:** writing a real vertical pack (it belongs in `packs/`, not in core), the web UI for choosing a pack, changing regulated-info behaviour.

## Staging of the work
S1 (builder, reviewer verifies): shared pack validation + tests; `agent-turn.ts` knowledge turn + unit tests with the fake provider (answered from a knowledge fact, price question hands over, empty search hands over, catalog pack unchanged, regulated turn unchanged, scheduling turn unchanged); a test-only knowledge-only pack fixture under the existing test helpers (not under `packs/`); the offline eval scenarios; ADR "S1 status"; AUDIT_TASK_BOARD AUD-09 and AUD-14 marked "implemented, awaiting reviewer verification".

## Risks
A knowledge turn answers from retrieved text: wrong passages are the main risk. Mitigations are in decisions 2-3 (facts with provenance only, handover when nothing is found, no commercial facts) and the eval set. The intent list used in decision 3 must come from the existing classifier output, not from keyword matching of the customer text (untrusted data).

## S1 status (2026-11-13, branch `feat/knowledge-only-tenants`, awaiting reviewer verification)

Built: decision 1 (`packages/shared/src/domain-pack.ts`); decisions 2-4 (`core/agent/agent-turn.ts`, `core/agent/tools.ts`: `ToolContext.connector` may be absent, then only `knowledge_search` can run); decision 5 log (`agent/agent-conversation.service.ts`, reasons `NO_CONNECTOR|AMBIGUOUS|UNAVAILABLE|PACK_HAS_NO_CATALOG`, no customer text); decision 6 (`agent/knowledge-only-eval.spec.ts`); test-only pack `agent/testing/knowledge-only-pack.ts`; unit tests `agent/knowledge-only-turns.spec.ts`. Intents that hand over come from the classifier output (`price`, `availability`, `booking_interest`).

Behaviour of a knowledge turn: price/availability/booking_interest -> `NO_DATA` without searching; other intents search the knowledge base with the customer text; at least one knowledge fact above the pack score -> reply rendered through the normal pipeline; empty, below-score or failed search -> `NO_DATA`; regulated_info -> `LEGAL_UNVERIFIED` (unchanged); scheduling not offered.

Deviations:
- A pack with only `knowledge_search` no longer needs a search slot (the "at least one slot required for search" rule applies only to packs with `search_catalog`); otherwise a knowledge-only pack could not validate.
- With no connector, tools are now built when the pack offers `knowledge_search` and a knowledge port exists. Scheduling is still passed only with a connector, and regulated questions still need a connector (kept as before; decision 7). A catalog pack that has `knowledge_search` and no usable catalog connector (education) now answers from knowledge instead of `NO_DATA` (decision 5).
- The offline eval scenarios are a separate spec, not JSON cases in `eval/cases`: those cases, the coverage gate and the live runner are tied to the education and travel packs.
- Not verified: the service log and the no-connector wiring through `AgentConversationService` (integration specs not run by the builder); no test covers scheduling-without-connector.
