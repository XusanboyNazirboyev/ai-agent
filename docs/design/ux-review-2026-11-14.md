# UX review: what is confusing for an admin or operator (2026-11-14)

Goal set by the owner: a simple interface that an ordinary admin or operator understands without training; first pilot customer is a **tourism** business.

**Method and limits.** Read from the code and the translation files (navigation, settings sections, the en and uz-Latn strings) on 2026-11-14. No screenshots, no real users, no timing. Every finding below is a hypothesis to confirm with the usability test at the end. Severity: H = blocks or misleads a new user, M = slows them down, L = polish.

## A. Structure and navigation
| ID | Finding | Evidence | Suggested fix | Sev | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Everyone sees the same five items: Inbox, Leads, **Analytics, Knowledge, Settings**. An operator needs two of them. | `NAV.map` in `app-shell.tsx` has no role filter (checked); `analytics-page.tsx` then shows an operator a "not allowed" message: a dead end reached from the menu. The Knowledge page's role handling was not checked. | Operator sees Inbox, Leads, Customers; Analytics, Knowledge, Settings only for OWNER and ADMIN. | H | S |
| A2 | Settings has **nine tabs** in one row (Bot, Channels, CRM, Lead stages, Fields, Team, Subscription, Security, General); the row scrolls sideways on a phone. | `SECTIONS` in `settings-section-nav.tsx`. | Group into 4: "Assistant" (bot + knowledge), "Channels", "Sales" (stages, fields, CRM), "Team and account" (team, subscription, security). | H | M |
| A3 | The assistant is split across two places: Settings, Bot (on/off, mode) and top-level Knowledge (what it knows). | nav + settings. | One "Assistant" area with tabs On/off, Knowledge, Test chat. | M | M |
| A4 | There is no home page that says what needs attention now. | the app opens on the Inbox. | A "Today" page: waiting customers, new leads, overdue, bot status as a traffic light, a getting-started checklist (3/5 done). | H | M |

| B2 | **"CRM" means two things**: the Leads page is itself the CRM, and "CRM integration" is the Bitrix24 link. | `nav.crm` "CRM integration", `settings.section.crm` "CRM". | Rename the tab to "Connect Bitrix24 / Boshqa tizimga ulash". | H | S |

| B4 | Technical words in front of non-technical users: **webhook**, **token**, "Web Inbox", "model call", "encryption key", `SECRETS_MASTER_KEY` in a user-visible message. | `channels.webhookUrl`, `connect.token`, `channels.needSecrets`, `botEffective.HOLD`. | Plain words ("bot kaliti"), hide technical steps behind "Details", never show env names to an admin (show "ask your technician"). | H | M |

## C. First-time setup (biggest barrier for a new customer)
| ID | Finding | Evidence | Suggested fix | Sev | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Getting started needs four separate settings screens in the right order (channel, knowledge, assistant mode, CRM). Nothing tells the admin the order. | settings sections. | A setup wizard with the checklist from A4: connect Telegram, upload prices/FAQ, test chat, switch on "drafts only", then "on". | H | L |
| C2 | Connecting Telegram asks for a bot token from BotFather without guidance. | `connect.token`. | Step-by-step screen with pictures and a "check" button (the test strings exist: `test.tokenValid`). | H | M |
| C3 | No way to try the assistant before real customers see it. | none. | "Test chat" inside the app (draft mode, no customer). Also the best demo tool. | H | M |
| C4 | Empty pages do not say what to do next. | several `*.empty` strings are one line without an action. | Each empty page: one sentence and one primary button. | M | S |
| C5 | Connector roles (Catalog, Lead source, Actions) are shown to every admin on the CRM page. | `connector-roles.tsx`. | Show only when two or more connectors exist; otherwise hide ("advanced"). | M | S |

## D. Operator daily work
| ID | Finding | Suggested fix | Sev | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | The operator writes every reply from scratch. | Assistant drafts a reply, one tap to send or edit; two-line summary of what the customer wants on handover. | H | L |
| D2 | No canned replies, assignment or reminders. | Roadmap P1.8 and P1.6; start with canned replies and "customer waiting too long" reminders. | M | M |

| D4 | The owner cannot see results without opening the app. | Daily Telegram summary (ADR 063). | M | M |

## E. What tourism needs (first pilot) and what is missing
- **A catalog from a price list.** There is no real catalog connector (staging uses `mock`). A tour agency has a price list in Excel or Google Sheets, not a CRM API. Without "upload prices as the catalog" the assistant cannot quote tours with verified facts (D1). **This is the first technical blocker for the pilot.**
- **Tourism templates:** pipeline `Inquiry, Offer sent, Prepayment, Documents/visa, Paid, Travelled` / `Lost`; fields destination, dates, travellers, budget, passport-needed; tags such as family, honeymoon, hot tour. The travel pack exists; the template and fields are not wired into onboarding.
- **PDF and Word itineraries** as knowledge (roadmap P2.7). Tour programs and rules live in documents.
- **Channels:** Telegram and Instagram are the main ones and work; voice notes are common in this market and are supported.
- **Currencies:** prices in USD and UZS; the system stores a currency per price, the amount field on leads is hidden by owner decision (revisit for tourism: a trip price is the core of a lead).
- **Seasonality and availability:** seats and dates per departure must come from the catalog, never from model memory.

## F. Proposed order (for discussion, nothing started)
1. Words and navigation quick wins: B1, B2, B3, B6, B7, A1, C4, C5 (small, mostly strings and menu filters; Kilo can take the i18n parts with a native review).
2. Today page and getting-started checklist (A4).
3. Catalog from Excel/Google Sheets (E, first bullet) in parallel: it unblocks the tourism pilot.
4. Setup wizard with Telegram guide and test chat (C1, C2, C3).
5. Assistant reply drafts and handover summary (D1).
6. Settings regrouping and the Assistant area (A2, A3).
7. Tourism template: stages, fields, tags, travel pack wiring.
8. Owner's daily Telegram report and conversation insights (ADR 063).
9. Usability test with 3 real people (an operator, an admin, an owner) on five tasks: add a lead, answer a waiting customer, switch the assistant off, add a price, find yesterday's new leads. Run it after step 2 and again after step 4.
